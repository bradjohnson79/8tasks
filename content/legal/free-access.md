8Tasks is always free to use, for everyone. This policy explains what that means and how 8Tasks is paid for.

## What is always free

- Browsing, searching and filtering every earning opportunity we list.
- Following our links to providers. You never need an 8Tasks account to see a listing or to visit a provider.
- Saving opportunities and searches, on your device or in an optional free account.
- My Daily 8 and opportunity alerts by email, when you choose to receive them.
- Optional free accounts, including preferences, personal mission and earnings tracking, data export and account deletion.

## What we will never do

- Charge for membership, premium tiers or "pro" features.
- Lock categories, listings or alerts behind a payment.
- Ask you to pay 8Tasks to apply to, join or be paid by a provider.
- Show different listings to people who pay, because nobody pays.

## How 8Tasks is paid for

Some providers pay us a referral commission when you join them through our links. That commission comes from the provider, never from you, and it never reduces what the provider pays you. Listings marked "Independent listing" earn 8Tasks no commission; we include them because they are useful. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/legal/affiliate-disclosure) for details.

## Costs set by providers

Providers are independent companies with their own terms. Some opportunities involve costs that the provider sets, such as a purchase that is later reimbursed, equipment you need, or the provider's own fees. Where we know about these, we show them on the listing. Always check the provider's terms before you start. Read our [Earning Disclaimer](/legal/earning-disclaimer) as well.

If anything on 8Tasks ever asks you to pay for access, please tell us at [8tasks@proton.me](mailto:8tasks@proton.me).
